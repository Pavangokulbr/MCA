# MoodPulse Classroom — Mockup

**Design canvas:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DrednvsF4joXCRWZon4mQn (private until shared from the canvas Share menu)

14 artboards, high fidelity, with design-rationale notes beside each screen. M1–M3 are one working prototype (press Play on M1).

| Persona | Screens |
|---|---|
| Overview | 00 Flows, mood scale, palette |
| Student (phone, no login, 390×844) | M1 Pick a mood · M2 Mood + pace selected · M3 Recorded anonymously · M4 Cooldown · M5 No lecture now |
| Faculty (desktop) | F1 Sign-in · F2 Today · F3 Lecture pulse timeline · F4 Course trend |
| Management (desktop) | A1 Department slot heatmap · A2 Classrooms & QR · A3 Room timetable · A4 Printed desk card (A6) |

**Visual language:** playful, high-saturation classroom style — lime / yellow / pink / sky / lilac grounds, black outlines and pill buttons, Plus Jakarta Sans 800 headlines. Black text on every colour.
**Mood scale (diverging, always text-labelled):** Lost `#C2410C` · Confused `#F08C2E` · Okay `#B9B5C4` · Following `#5E8FEA` · Engaged `#1F4FBF`.

**Assumptions drawn into the mockup (confirm before building):**
- Anti-spam = one tap per device per 10 min via a random daily device code (PROJECT.md decision #3). Does not stop off-room scans from a leaked QR photo; QR regeneration (A2) is the mitigation.
- Buckets with < 5 taps hidden; management sees slots/rooms, never faculty rankings.

> All names, courses and numbers are **sample data**. They are not test evidence — Chapter 7–8 data must come from a real class session.
