# 01 Formatting

## Mandatory Format

1. Submission mode: softcopy.
2. Page size: A4.
3. Font:
   - Body: Times New Roman, 12
   - Headings: 14
   - Subheadings: 13
4. Margins:
   - Left: 4 cm
   - Top: 3 cm
   - Bottom: 3 cm
   - Right: 2 cm
5. Fresh paragraph starts after five spaces.
6. Line spacing: double spacing or 1.5 spacing.
7. Page number position: bottom-middle.
8. Do not underline headings/subheadings.
9. Do not use colons in headings/subheadings.
10. Minimum pages: 30.
